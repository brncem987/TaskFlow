Welcome 👋

CIRBE Control reads the CIRBE files you exchange with Banco de España — the ones you send, and the replies that come back — and tells you in plain language which records were accepted, which were rejected, and what to do about each one. Nothing leaves your computer.

① It reads your filesPoint it at your two folders. It opens each CIRBE / EDITRAN file and understands the records inside.
② It explains every resultEach record is shown as Accepted, Rejected, or Pending — with the error code translated into plain English and a suggested fix.
③ It tracks the full storySent, rejected, resent, finally accepted? Click any record to see its whole timeline and its current state.

Error help — what each result means

A plain-language dictionary of Banco de España result codes. Each says why you got it and how to fix it. Don’t see a code? Add its meaning once and it will be applied everywhere.

Rejected the record was not loaded — fix and resend
Pending loaded but inconsistent — correct before month close
Accepted all good
Info a notice or warning — usually no action

Train detection

These are result codes that appear in your files but don’t yet have an exact meaning — they’re currently classified only by their family rule (R/L/A…) or not at all. Teach the ones you see most often; every matching record updates instantly and stays taught.
